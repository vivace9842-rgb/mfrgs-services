Our Methodology

A structured process, not a database query

Every MFRGS report follows a disciplined eight-step methodology — combining automated data retrieval with human analyst judgment at every stage.

01

Identity Verification

We confirm the legal identity of the subject — name, registration, jurisdiction, and ownership structure — against official sources.

02

Registry Validation

Corporate status, filing history, and registered addresses are validated directly against national business registries.

03

Litigation Research

We search court records, regulatory filings, and legal databases for active or historical litigation and judgments.

04

Reputation Analysis

Media monitoring, adverse news screening, and digital reputation analysis conducted across multiple languages.

05

OSINT Intelligence

Open-source intelligence techniques map digital presence, social profiles, domain history and public connections.

06

Risk Assessment

All findings are scored across financial, legal, reputational and operational risk dimensions.

07

Human Analyst Review

A certified analyst reviews all data, resolves ambiguities and applies professional judgment before finalization.

08

Final Executive Report

A structured PDF is produced — executive summary, risk matrix, evidence appendix and analyst recommendation.
