Our Methodology

A structured process, not a database query

Every MFRGS report follows a disciplined eight-step methodology — combining automated data retrieval with human analyst judgment at every stage.

01

Identity Verification

We confirm the legal identity of the subject — name, registration, jurisdiction, and ownership structure — against official sources.

02

Registry Validation

Corporate status, filing history, and registered addresses are validated directly against national business registries and official databases.

03

Litigation Research

We search court records, regulatory filings, and legal databases for active or historical litigation, judgments, and regulatory actions.

04

Reputation Analysis

Media monitoring, adverse news screening, and digital reputation analysis are conducted across multiple languages and jurisdictions.

05

OSINT Intelligence

Open-source intelligence techniques are applied to map digital presence, social profiles, domain history, and publicly available network connections.

06

Risk Assessment

All findings are scored across financial, legal, reputational, and operational risk dimensions using our structured risk matrix framework.

07

Human Analyst Review

A certified analyst reviews all data, resolves ambiguities, cross-references sources, and applies professional judgment before the report is finalized.

08

Final Executive Report

A structured PDF is produced — executive summary, risk matrix, evidence appendix, and a clear analyst recommendation — ready for professional use.
