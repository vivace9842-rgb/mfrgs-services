Identity & Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions. Have questions regarding the exact scope of MFRGS Digital Verification? Review our detailed parameters below.

Where do the records in the report originate?

Exclusively from official public corporate registries, tax and regulatory authorities, government gazettes, and court systems in the jurisdiction where the entity is registered.

Does MFRGS offer guaranteed fraud protection?

No report can guarantee the absence of fraud. We provide an objective analysis of public data to support your own decision, not a fraud-proof guarantee.

How are reports formatted and delivered?

Reports are delivered as a structured executive PDF document, sent by email within the turnaround window stated for each plan.

Does MFRGS provide legal counsel or tax advisory?

No. Our reports are objective technical evaluations, not legal, tax, or financial advice. Consult qualified professionals for those matters.

What are the limitations of MFRGS' liability?

Reports reflect what is officially on public record at the time of verification. MFRGS is not liable for outcomes of transactions or decisions made using the report; final contractual and financial choices remain the ordering company's sole responsibility.
