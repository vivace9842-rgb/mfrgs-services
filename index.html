What We Do

What We Do to Protect Your Commercial Transactions

MFRGS fills the gap between risky informal deals and traditional due diligence pricing, using structured public registers and open-source intelligence (OSINT).

01

Supplier & Vendor Verification

Avoid wiring deposits to inactive entities or mismatched commercial addresses. We check official corporate registrations, tax standing, and operating filings to verify operational validity.

02

Reputational Audit & OSINT

We analyze digital footprints, local news indexes, business dispute publications, and public complaint databases to build a clear picture of the counterparty's track record.

03

Litigation & Registry History

We compile active civil, tax, and labor court records from the relevant national or regional court systems, helping you identify regulatory burdens or pending liabilities.

Scope & Compliance Memorandum MFRGS compiles intelligence relying strictly on verified government registries and open-access public records. We do not provide legal advice, financial counsel, formal accounting audits, or private detective/investigative services. Our reports consolidate available public records to support safer commercial choices, under the ordering company's sole discretion and final business responsibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do the records in the report originate?

All information is gathered exclusively from official state registries, government portals, court systems, and verified open-source intelligence databases corresponding to the company's registration jurisdiction.

No. We provide objective OSINT intelligence and registry facts to significantly lower your blind spots, but commercial transactions carry inherent business risks under the ordering company's final discretion.

How are reports formatted and delivered?

Reports are compiled into clean, structured executive PDF dossiers sent directly to your designated email address within the turnaround timeline specified in your chosen plan.

Does MFRGS provide legal counsel or tax advisory?

No. MFRGS is an intelligence provider, not a law firm or accounting consultancy. Our findings support your commercial risk evaluation process.
