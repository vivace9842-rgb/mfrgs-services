OUR BLUEPRINT

The MFRGS Verification Method

1

Ask

You submit the target company, website, email address, or document you received. We start analyzing your specific doubt.

2

Verify

We poll official government databases (like the UK Companies House) to confirm existence, legal registration, history, and active directors.

3

Analyse

We examine behaviour: domain age, email authentication (SPF/DKIM), digital presence, and high-risk signals like extreme urgency or off-platform payment pressure.

4

Classify

We cross-reference all risk parameters to label the company with an objective risk rating: Low, Medium, High, or Critical.

5

Conclude

You receive a direct, verified PDF dossier telling you exactly if you should proceed, negotiate safely, or run away.
